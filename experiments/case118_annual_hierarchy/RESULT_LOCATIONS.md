# Annual study result locations

The maintained saved-data dashboard and analysis tools are in [analysis/](analysis/README.md).
The accepted scientific record is [S5_REPORT.md](S5_REPORT.md), `S5_RESULTS.json`,
and `s5_closeout/`. Selected tracked evidence is in `analysis/artifacts/`.

The following ignored locations retain historical originals:

- `results/s5_analysis/`: original dashboard/analyzers, snapshots, and closeout records.
- `results/analysis/`: interim timing collectors, plots, and exploratory source.
- `results/s5_coverage/`: original coverage source, tests, and reports.
- `results/s5_authority/`, `results/diagnostics/`, and `results/S5_OPERATOR_RUNBOOK.md`:
  historical execution authority, recovery evidence, and operator notes.

Archived scripts are evidence of earlier implementations, not maintained entry
points. Their old root calculations/imports may require the historical layout.
Do not repair or run them as substitutes for the promoted tools.

The [disposition](S5_ARTIFACT_DISPOSITION.md), [inventory](analysis/output_inventory.csv),
and [promotion manifest](analysis/promotion_manifest.json) explain intentional
copies and adapted counterparts. Preserve these original records and hashes.
`RESULT_LOCATIONS_20260921.json` records exact old/current file locations and
relocation hashes. Historical paths embedded in evidence remain unchanged.
The owner approved retaining the relocated material here after reviewing the
broader move; the original root-folder rename did not authorize that move.

New outputs from maintained annual analysis tools go to fresh directories under
`results/reproductions/`, or an explicit output override. Existing raw S4/S5
execution archives remain in their established `results/` subtrees. No raw
archive is promoted or regenerated by the filesystem repair.
